Speech Acts theo Renkema & Schubert (2018) được định nghĩa là gì?
"All expressions of language must be viewed as acts."
"All expressions of language must be viewed as sentences."
"All expressions of language must be viewed as meanings."
"All expressions of language must be viewed as utterances."
Constatives là gì?
Statements that can be true or false, describing states of affairs.
Utterances that perform an action by saying something.
Utterances that indicate the speaker's psychological attitude.
Utterances that bring about an immediate correspondence between content and reality.
Performatives là gì?
Utterances that perform an action — do something by saying it (e.g., promise, apologize).
Statements that can be verified as true or false.
Utterances that describe states of affairs in the world.
Utterances that commit the speaker to something's being true.
Locutionary act là gì?
The form — literally what is said (the sentence itself).
The force or function — what is meant by the utterance.
The outcome or effect the utterance has on the hearer.
The condition under which an utterance is considered felicitous.
Illocutionary act là gì?
The force or function — what is meant by the utterance.
The form — literally what is said.
The outcome or effect produced on the hearer.
The condition for the utterance to be successfully performed.
Perlocutionary act là gì?
The outcome or effect of the utterance on the hearer — NOTE: may NOT be fulfilled.
The form or literal meaning of what is said.
The intended function the speaker wants to convey.
The condition under which the act is considered sincere.
Felicity conditions là gì?
Conditions requiring the communication be carried out by the right person, in the right place, at the right time, with a certain intention.
Conditions determining whether an utterance is true or false.
Conditions describing the grammatical structure of a performative verb.
Conditions requiring the hearer to respond appropriately to a speech act.
Nếu điều kiện felicity thứ nhất hoặc thứ hai không được thỏa mãn, điều gì xảy ra?
The act will not be achieved — it will "misfire."
The act will be considered insincere — it will be "abused."
The act will be repaired by the hearer automatically.
The act will be reclassified as a constative statement.
Nếu điều kiện felicity thứ ba không được thỏa mãn, điều gì xảy ra?
The procedure will be "abused."
The act will "misfire" and not be achieved.
The act becomes a performative automatically.
The act loses its locutionary content entirely.
Điều kiện propositional content của lời hứa (promise) là gì?
Speakers commit to carry out a future act by themselves; one cannot promise something already done.
Speakers must honestly be willing to fulfill the promise.
Speakers take responsibility for carrying out the act stated.
Speakers must have the authority and the right to make the promise.
Điều kiện sincerity của lời hứa là gì?
Speakers must honestly be willing to fulfill the promise even if they are not willing at the moment.
Speakers must carry out a future act by themselves.
Speakers take responsibility for the act stated in the promise.
Speakers must perform the promise in the correct social setting.
IFIDS là gì và bao gồm những gì?
Illocutionary Force Indicating Devices — include performative verbs, word order, intonation, accent, certain adverbs, and the mode of the verb.
Illocutionary Force Indicating Devices — include only intonation and word order.
Indirect Force Indicating Devices — include context, background knowledge, and implication.
Illocutionary Function Identifying Devices — include only performative verbs and sentence mode.
Khi IFIDS hiện diện (present), illocution của phát ngôn là gì?
Explicit illocution.
Implicit illocution.
Indirect illocution.
Absent illocution.
Khi IFIDS không hiện diện, illocution của phát ngôn là gì?
Implicit or indirect illocution.
Explicit illocution.
Direct illocution.
Absent illocution.
Theo Searle's taxonomy, Representatives là gì?
Commit the speaker to something's being true (e.g., assert, claim, report, state).
Attempts by the speaker to get the hearer to do something.
Commit the speaker to some future course of action.
Bring about an immediate correspondence between content and reality.
Theo Searle's taxonomy, Directives là gì?
Attempts by the speaker to get the hearer to do something (e.g., ask, order, command, request).
Commit the speaker to something's being true.
Convey the speaker's psychological attitude toward a state of affairs.
Commit the speaker to some future course of action.
Theo Searle's taxonomy, Commissives là gì?
Commit the speaker to some future course of action (e.g., promise, offer, threaten, pledge).
Commit the speaker to something's being true.
Attempts by the speaker to get the hearer to do something.
Bring about correspondence between content and reality.
Theo Searle's taxonomy, Expressives là gì?
Convey the speaker's psychological attitude toward a state of affairs (e.g., thank, congratulate, apologize, welcome).
Commit the speaker to some future course of action.
Bring about immediate correspondence between content and reality.
Attempts to get the hearer to do something.
Theo Searle's taxonomy, Declarations là gì?
Bring about an immediate correspondence between the expressed content and reality (e.g., excommunicate, nominate, sentence).
Commit the speaker to something's being true.
Convey the speaker's psychological attitude toward a state of affairs.
Commit the speaker to some future course of action.
Phát ngôn "Give me a hand" có directness như thế nào?
Direct — it is an imperative sentence realizing a request directly.
Conventionally indirect — it is an interrogative asking for ability.
Non-conventionally indirect — it is a declarative used as a request.
Implicit — it is a constative describing a situation.
Phát ngôn "Can you give me a hand?" có directness như thế nào?
Conventionally indirect — interrogative sentence used as a request.
Direct — it is a direct command using imperative mood.
Non-conventionally indirect — it is a declarative used as a request.
Explicit — it uses a performative verb to indicate the illocution.
Grice's Cooperative Principle được phát biểu như thế nào?
Make your conversational contribution such as is required, at the stage at which it occurs, by the accepted purpose or direction of the speech exchange in which you are engaged.
Make your conversational contribution as brief, true, and relevant as possible at all times.
Make sure every utterance you produce is true, sincere, and free of ambiguity.
Contribute to conversation only when you are certain of the truth of what you say.
Maxim of Quality yêu cầu điều gì?
Be true — do not say what you believe to be false or what you lack evidence for.
Be as informative as required — do not say more or less than needed.
Be brief and avoid ambiguity — be orderly and clear in your contribution.
Be relevant — make your contribution relevant to the current exchange.
Maxim of Quantity yêu cầu điều gì?
Be as informative as required — neither too much nor too little.
Be true — only say what you have evidence for.
Be relevant — keep your contribution related to the topic.
Be brief and avoid obscurity — do not be unnecessarily wordy.
Maxim of Manner yêu cầu điều gì?
Be brief, orderly, and avoid ambiguity — be perspicuous.
Be as informative as required — say enough but not too much.
Be true — only assert what you believe and have evidence for.
Be relevant — make your contribution fit the purpose of the exchange.
Maxim of Relevance yêu cầu điều gì?
Be relevant — make your contribution pertinent to the current conversation.
Be brief — avoid unnecessary prolixity.
Be true — do not assert falsehoods.
Be informative — provide all the necessary information.
"Observe the maxims" có nghĩa là gì?
Straightforwardly obey the maxims without deviation.
Fail to observe a maxim inconspicuously/unnoticeably.
Fail to observe a maxim blatantly/obviously.
Refuse to play the conversational game at all.
"Violate a maxim" có nghĩa là gì?
Fail to observe a maxim inconspicuously/unnoticeably — covertly break the maxim.
Straightforwardly obey all the maxims.
Fail to observe a maxim blatantly and obviously.
Refuse to engage in the conversation at all.
"Flout a maxim" có nghĩa là gì?
Fail to observe a maxim blatantly/obviously — the hearer is meant to notice.
Fail to observe a maxim inconspicuously so the hearer does not notice.
Straightforwardly obey all maxims in the exchange.
Refuse to play the conversational game entirely.
"Opt out of the maxims" có nghĩa là gì?
Refuse to play the conversational game at all (remain silent or go off-topic).
Fail to observe a maxim blatantly so the hearer notices.
Fail to observe a maxim inconspicuously.
Obey the maxims strictly in every utterance.
Conversational implicatures được suy ra dựa trên những yếu tố nào?
The conventional meaning of words, the cooperative principle and its maxims, linguistic and non-linguistic context, and background knowledge.
The grammatical structure of sentences and the lexical choices of the speaker only.
The illocutionary force indicating devices and the performative verbs used.
The felicity conditions and the sincerity conditions of the speech act.
Phương pháp của Conversation Analysis (CA) là gì?
CA is highly empirical and data-driven, beginning with audio/video recordings; the transcription of recordings is also the analysis.
CA uses introspective judgments about grammaticality and acceptability of spoken language.
CA relies primarily on experimental lab methods and controlled elicitation tasks.
CA analyses written texts to uncover the underlying conversational structures they represent.
Turn trong Conversation Analysis là gì?
A part of spoken discourse by one speaker, after the speech of another and before others take over again; can vary from a single word to a complete story.
The entire conversation between two or more participants from opening to closing.
The moment at which one speaker finishes and another is expected to begin speaking.
The syntactic unit — sentence, phrase, or word — that makes up a single utterance.
Turn-Constructional Unit (TCU) là gì?
The part of an utterance that might be a complete turn, after which another speaker might take over; built up of syntactical units.
The entire set of turns produced by one speaker throughout a conversation.
The point in a turn where transfer of the floor becomes relevant.
The device speakers use to signal that they want to take the next turn.
Transition-Relevance Place (TRP) là gì?
The moment in the production of a turn at which that turn might be possibly complete and another speaker might take over.
The unit of utterance that constitutes a possible complete turn.
The sequence of turns that forms a coherent conversational exchange.
The strategy a speaker uses to hold the floor against potential interruption.
Quy tắc 1a của turn-taking là gì?
If the current speaker uses a "current speaker selects next" technique, the selected participant has the right and obligation to take the next turn.
If no one is selected, the current speaker may continue unless someone self-selects.
If no one is selected, self-selection for next speakership may be instituted.
If the initial TRP is reached, the rule-set re-applies at the next TRP.
Quy tắc 1b của turn-taking là gì?
If the current speaker does NOT select next, self-selection may be instituted — the first person to start acquires the right to the turn.
If the current speaker selects next, that person has the right and obligation to speak.
If no one self-selects, the current speaker must continue.
If the TRP is reached and no one speaks, the conversation ends.
Adjacency pair là gì?
A two-part sequence in which the first part makes relevant the production of a second pair-part of the type suggested by the first part, following immediately after.
A pair of turns in which both speakers say the same thing at the same time.
A sequence of three or more turns forming a coherent conversational episode.
A turn in which one speaker produces both a question and its answer.
FPP và SPP trong adjacency pairs là gì?
FPP = First Pair Part (e.g., question, invitation); SPP = Second Pair Part (e.g., answer, acceptance) — SPP is expected after FPP.
FPP = Final Pair Part that closes the sequence; SPP = Secondary Pair Part that optionally follows.
FPP = Formal Pragmatic Particle; SPP = Secondary Pragmatic Particle used in discourse.
FPP = First Preferred Part; SPP = Second Preferred Part of a conversational exchange.
Preferred response là gì?
The quick, unmarked, simple response — e.g., direct acceptance of an invitation.
The delayed, marked, and sometimes complicated response.
The response that neither speaker actually desires to occur.
The response that requires additional explanation or accounts.
Dispreferred response là gì?
The delayed, marked, sometimes complicated response — e.g., a rejection or refusal with accounts.
The quick, unmarked, and simple response to an adjacency pair.
The response that both speakers prefer to avoid in polite conversation.
The response that immediately follows the first pair part without hesitation.
Pre-expansion là gì?
A sequence that comes before the base adjacency pair, often to prepare the ground for the FPP (e.g., a pre-invitation).
A sequence inserted between the FPP and SPP of the base pair.
A sequence that follows the SPP to elaborate on or assess the response.
A sequence that replaces the base adjacency pair entirely.
Insert expansion là gì?
A sequence inserted between the FPP and SPP that temporarily suspends the base pair.
A sequence that comes before the FPP to prepare for the main exchange.
A sequence that follows the SPP to close down the sequence.
A sequence that replaces the SPP when a dispreferred response is given.
Post-expansion là gì?
A sequence that follows the SPP of the base pair, extending or closing the sequence.
A sequence inserted between FPP and SPP.
A sequence that precedes the FPP to check conditions for the main act.
A sequence that replaces the FPP when the speaker changes their mind.
Discourse markers trong CA là gì?
Pragmatic particles in spoken communication that have expressive functions; forms include connectives, adverbs, prepositional phrases, and minimal clauses.
Grammatical words that connect clauses only through semantic meaning.
Lexical items that indicate the topic of the conversation.
Devices used exclusively in written discourse to organize paragraphs.
"Face" theo Brown & Levinson là gì?
An image of self delineated in terms of approved social attributes.
The literal expression on a person's face during social interaction.
The set of strategies speakers use to avoid conflict in conversation.
The social distance between speakers in any given interaction.
Positive face là gì?
The need to be appreciated by others — the want for approval from others.
The need to be free and not be interfered with by others.
The strategy of emphasizing avoidance and distance in interaction.
The desire to perform face-threatening acts without social cost.
Negative face là gì?
The need to be free and not be interfered with — the want not to have one's freedom of action restricted.
The need to be appreciated and approved of by others.
The strategy of closeness and solidarity used in positive politeness.
The desire to threaten others' face in order to exercise power.
FTA (Face Threatening Act) là gì?
An action that can form a threat to the other's positive or negative face.
A strategy used to preserve the positive face of the hearer.
A technique for increasing the weight of politeness in interaction.
An utterance that simultaneously threatens and repairs face.
Công thức tính W(FTA) là gì?
W(FTA) = R + D + P (Rate of imposition + social Distance + Power).
W(FTA) = R × D × P (Rate × Distance × Power).
W(FTA) = D + P − R (Distance + Power minus Rate of imposition).
W(FTA) = P + R − D (Power + Rate minus Distance).
Threats to positive face (affective impoliteness) là gì?
Conveying the speaker's negative feelings towards the hearer — e.g., insults, criticism.
Used by the speaker to exercise power over the hearer coercively.
Entertainment-based face damage aimed at an audience.
Strategies that minimize the imposition on the hearer's freedom.
Threats to negative face (coercive impoliteness) là gì?
Used by the speaker to exercise power — imposing on the hearer's freedom of action.
Conveying the speaker's negative feelings towards the hearer affectively.
Entertaining face damage directed at a third-party audience.
Strategies that show solidarity and closeness between participants.
Solidarity politeness là gì?
Face work aimed at positive face, characterized by the emphasis of closeness and solidarity.
Face work aimed at negative face, characterized by avoidance-based strategies.
The explicit rudeness that maximizes face damage for entertainment.
The strategy of doing FTAs baldly without redressive action.
Respect politeness là gì?
Face work aimed at negative face, characterized by the emphasis of avoidance-based strategies.
Face work aimed at positive face, emphasizing closeness and solidarity.
The strategy of performing FTAs on record with positive politeness.
The technique of opting out entirely to avoid committing a FTA.
Impoliteness là gì?
The explicit rudeness that boosts or maximizes face damage.
Any behaviour used to constitute and maintain one's own face.
Face work aimed at the hearer's positive face through solidarity.
The choice to avoid performing a FTA altogether.
Ba chức năng communicative chính của impoliteness là gì?
Affective impoliteness, coercive impoliteness, and entertaining impoliteness.
Solidarity impoliteness, respect impoliteness, and neutral impoliteness.
Direct impoliteness, indirect impoliteness, and implicit impoliteness.
Positive impoliteness, negative impoliteness, and off-record impoliteness.
Chiến lược "Do the FTA on record without redressive action, baldly" là gì?
Performing the FTA directly and openly without any mitigation (Strategy 1).
Performing the FTA with positive politeness strategies to soften it.
Performing the FTA off record so the hearer must infer the meaning.
Choosing not to perform the FTA at all to preserve face.
Chiến lược "Don't do the FTA" là gì?
Choosing not to perform the face-threatening act at all — complete avoidance.
Performing the FTA baldly without any softening strategies.
Performing the FTA off record through hints and implicature.
Performing the FTA with negative politeness to minimize imposition.
